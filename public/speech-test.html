Camera Coach

Speech test: checks whether captions can use your microphone and run the speech model inside Webex. Your audio stays on this computer.

– 1. Microphone

Click Start microphone and say something. If asked, allow microphone access.

– 2. Speech model

Click Load speech model. The first time, it downloads about 28 MB; after that it's cached.

– 3. Speech to text

With the microphone on and the model loaded, click Record 5 seconds and talk.
